About Waterlogging in Dhaka

Dhaka experiences severe waterlogging during monsoon (June–October). Reporting helps authorities prioritize areas.

Dengue Prevention Tips
  • ✅ Remove standing water from containers weekly
  • ✅ Cover water storage tanks and drums
  • ✅ Clear blocked drains near your home
  • ✅ Use mosquito nets and repellents
  • ✅ Report breeding sites using this app
Waste Management
  • ✅ Separate wet and dry waste at home
  • ✅ Never dump garbage in drains or canals
  • ✅ Report illegal dumping spots
  • ✅ Recycle plastic, paper, and glass
