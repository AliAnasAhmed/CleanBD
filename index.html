About Waterlogging in Dhaka

Dhaka experiences severe waterlogging during monsoon (June–October). Poor drainage, filled canals, and unplanned urbanization contribute to the problem. Reporting helps authorities prioritize areas for improvement.

Dengue Prevention Tips
  • ✅ Remove standing water from containers weekly
  • ✅ Cover water storage tanks and drums
  • ✅ Clear blocked drains near your home
  • ✅ Dispose of unused tires that collect rainwater
  • ✅ Use mosquito nets and repellents
  • ✅ Report breeding sites using this app
  • ✅ Wear long-sleeved clothes during dawn/dusk
  • ✅ Clean flower vases and pet bowls regularly
Waste Management
  • ✅ Separate wet and dry waste at home
  • ✅ Never dump garbage in drains or canals
  • ✅ Report illegal dumping spots
  • ✅ Participate in community clean-up drives
  • ✅ Recycle plastic, paper, and glass
  • ✅ Compost organic waste if possible
  • ✅ Dispose of medical waste properly
During Floods
  • ⚠️ Avoid walking through floodwater (risk of electrocution, disease)
  • ⚠️ Do not drink contaminated water
  • ⚠️ Keep emergency numbers handy
  • ⚠️ Store dry food and clean water
  • ⚠️ Report dangerous areas to help others
  • ⚠️ Watch for open manholes under water

In Case of Emergency

If you or someone nearby is in danger due to flooding, dengue outbreak, or hazardous waste — call 999 immediately. This is the national unified emergency number for all services.

About CleanBD

CleanBD is a community-powered environmental reporting platform designed for the citizens of Dhaka, Bangladesh. Our mission is to empower people to report environmental hazards — waterlogging, dengue breeding sites, and illegal garbage dumps — so that communities stay informed and authorities can take action.

All reports are stored locally in your browser. No personal data is collected. This is an open community tool for a cleaner, safer Bangladesh.

Your Data

Reports are saved in your browser's local storage. You can export or clear them anytime.
