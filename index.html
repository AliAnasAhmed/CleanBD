About Waterlogging in Dhaka

Dhaka experiences severe waterlogging during monsoon (June–October). Reporting helps authorities prioritize areas.

Dengue Prevention Tips
  • ✅ Remove standing water from containers weekly
  • ✅ Cover water storage tanks and drums
  • ✅ Clear blocked drains near your home
  • ✅ Use mosquito nets and repellents
  • ✅ Report breeding sites using this app
Waste Management
  • ✅ Separate wet and dry waste at home
  • ✅ Never dump garbage in drains or canals
  • ✅ Report illegal dumping spots
  • ✅ Recycle plastic, paper, and glass

About CleanBD

Empowering citizens to build a cleaner, safer, and more resilient Bangladesh through community-powered environmental reporting.

Our Mission

Dhaka faces recurring waterlogging, dengue outbreaks, and unmanaged waste every year. CleanBD gives citizens a simple way to report these hazards, track their resolution, and hold the process accountable — turning scattered complaints into visible, mappable community data.

We believe that when citizens are empowered with the right tools, they can drive meaningful change. CleanBD is that tool — a bridge between concerned citizens and the authorities who can make a difference.

How It Works

Reports are submitted with location, photos, and details, then verified and discussed by the community. Contributors earn reputation points and badges for reporting, verifying, and helping resolve issues.

1

Report

Submit a report with location, photos, and detailed description of the hazard

2

Verify

Community members verify the report to confirm its accuracy

3

Track

Follow the report's progress as it moves through resolution stages

4

Resolve

Mark the issue as resolved when it's fixed, earning reputation points

Your Data & Privacy

Privacy First

CleanBD stores everything locally in your browser through a lightweight simulated API layer. Nothing is sent to an external server, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

  • All reports and user data are stored in your browser's local storage
  • No personal information is transmitted to external servers
  • You have full control over your data — export or delete anytime
  • Anonymous browsing is available without creating an account
  • We never track your location without explicit permission
  • Your privacy is our priority — always
